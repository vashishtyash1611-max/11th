What is NIOS

India’s Open School, Explained Simply.

NIOS is an autonomous institution under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, offering Secondary (Class 10) and Senior Secondary (Class 12) certification through flexible, learner-paced study — with public exams, on-demand exams and credit-transfer routes.

  • Secondary & Senior Secondary certification with a minimum of five subjects
  • Two admission blocks every year plus On-Demand Exams when you are ready
  • Transfer of Credit for subjects already passed in another recognized board
  • Certification accepted for higher education and employment — confirm your target institution’s rules during counselling

NIOS Routes

Pick the Route That Fits Your Story

Fresh start, compartment, dropout comeback or mark improvement — NIOS has a defined route for each.

Secondary · Class 10

Equivalent to Class 10 with a minimum of five subjects. Minimum age 14 years; Class 8 pass certificate or self-certificate needed.

Senior Secondary · Class 12

Equivalent to Class 12 with a minimum of five subjects including at least one language. Needs a passed Class 10 from a recognized board; minimum age 15 years.

Two Blocks a Year

Stream 1 admissions run in two blocks with public exams in April–May and October–November, so a missed session never costs you a full year.

On-Demand Exams

Streams 2–4 let eligible learners appear when prepared instead of waiting for the fixed public-exam cycle — ideal for compartment and improvement cases.

Transfer of Credit

Passed subjects from another recognized board can transfer — up to two subjects passed in recent years — so you only re-appear for what is pending.

TMA + Practicals

Tutor Marked Assignments and study-centre practicals carry real weight. We drill both alongside theory so no marks leak in evaluation.

Official fee snapshot (Stream 1, five subjects)

Secondary — about Rs. 2,340 (male) / Rs. 1,890 (female) / Rs. 1,560 (SC·ST and eligible categories); Senior Secondary — about Rs. 2,600 / Rs. 2,150 / Rs. 1,690; plus the online form cost, with additional-subject and credit-transfer charges as published. Figures follow the NIOS student portal and change with official notifications — confirm the current structure with our counsellors before applying.

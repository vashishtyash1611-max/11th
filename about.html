About Genius IAS

An Institute Built on Mentorship, Discipline & Results.

Genius IAS is a Rohtak-based coaching centre offering UPSC IAS & HCS preparation — prelims, mains and interview — plus foundation coaching for school and college students, and the School of Humanities integrated program for Class 11th & 12th. Online and offline options are available.

Mission & Vision

Focused Preparation. Personal Attention.

Our mission is to provide focused, structured and result-oriented preparation for aspirants aiming for the Civil Services and allied examinations — blending rigorous academics with personal mentorship.

Our vision extends beyond exams: through the School of Humanities, we shape Class 11th & 12th students for CLAT, IPM, CUET, TISS, SAT, SET and the UPSC Foundation alongside the CBSE curriculum — building habits of discipline, analysis and expression early.

“Built for aspirants. Designed for success.”

5 Years · BBA + MBA
IPM 01

IIM Indore — Integrated Programme in Management

The original five-year IPM for students passing Class 12 — a head start in management with world-class IIM faculty, the brand value of an IIM graduate alongside graduation, and stronger employability through a wider training horizon. Worth attempting even alongside a bachelor’s elsewhere.

~150 SeatsIPMAT + InterviewResidential Program
Prepare for IIM Indore

Intake

Around 150 seats in recent admission cycles. Confirm the current cycle’s intake during counselling.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Passed Class XII (or appearing in the admission year) with qualifying years as notified
  • Passed Class X / SSC
  • Age bar as notified each cycle, with relaxation for SC / ST / PwD candidates

Admission Process

  • IPMAT (IIM Indore) aptitude test followed by Personal Interview
  • Final merit in recent cycles: roughly 65% test score + 35% interview

Gender Diversity

No separate published gender-diversity weightage for IPM — selection rests on the aptitude test and interview. Confirm the current cycle’s policy during counselling.

Why IPM at Indore?

  • Better management training thanks to a wider horizon and world-class IIM faculty
  • The brand value of being an IIM graduate alongside your graduation
  • Much improved employability versus traditional graduation options
  • Relatively easier entrance test compared to post-graduation management entrances
198 Seats · BBA + MBA
IPM 02

IIM Rohtak — Integrated Programme in Management

Launched in 2019 for students after Class XII aiming at business management. Completing five years earns an MBA from IIM Rohtak; exiting after three years earns a BBA.

IPMAT + InterviewBBA Exit Option+4 / −1 Marking
Prepare for IIM Rohtak

Intake

198 seats (including EWS) in the 2025 admission cycle. Confirm the current cycle’s intake during counselling.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Minimum 60% (55% for SC / ST / DAP) in Class X and Class XII
  • Maximum 20 years of age as of June 30 of the admission year, with relaxation for reserved categories as notified

Admission Process

  • IPM Aptitude Test — QA, Logical Reasoning and Verbal Ability (40 questions each, 120 minutes, +4 / −1, all MCQ)
  • Personal Interview for shortlisted candidates; final merit — aptitude test 55%, interview 15%, Class X 10%, Class XII 20%

Gender Diversity

Rohtak formally recognizes gender diversity in admissions, and recent IPM batches have recorded very strong female representation. Confirm the current cycle’s diversity weightage during counselling.

5 Years · BBA-MBA
IPM 03

IIM Ranchi — Integrated Programme in Management

A full-time 5-year blended business-studies course from undergraduate to postgraduate level (integrated BBA-MBA), awarding an MBA at the end of the programme.

IPMAT + InterviewAcademics 20%+5 Gender Bonus
Prepare for IIM Ranchi

Intake

Around 120 seats in recent admission cycles, under Government of India reservation policy. Confirm the current cycle’s intake during counselling.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Minimum 60% aggregate typically required in Class X and XII, with GoI relaxations — confirm the current cycle
  • Separate online application on the IIM Ranchi website is mandatory alongside the aptitude test

Admission Process

  • Aptitude test (IPMAT) 50% — 25% verbal ability + 25% quant — plus Personal Interview 30% and academic profile 20%
  • Academics scored on SSC and HSC slabs, topped by the interview-stage merit list

Gender Diversity

5 bonus marks added to the composite score for female and transgender candidates (added to the score, not the total).

5 Years · 15 Terms
IPM 07

NALSAR DoMS — Integrated Programme in Management

A unique five-year IPM producing legally aware managers — management education embedded with law, best-in-class faculty and dialogue with business and thought leaders. Fifteen terms: three years of theoretical foundations across philosophy, psychology, mathematics, economics, law and management, then two years of transformation into business leaders. Voluntary BBA exit after three years.

BBA + MBALaw + ManagementBBA Exit Option
Prepare for NALSAR IPM

Intake

Limited seats each cycle. Confirm the current intake during counselling.

  • Valid CLAT (UG) / IPMAT Indore / IPMAT Rohtak / JIPMAT / JEE (Mains) scorecard

IPM 08

IIFT Kakinada — Integrated Programme in Management

A five-year IPM integrating management with STEM — BBA (Business Analytics) after three years and MBA (International Business) after five. Semester system for three years, trimester system for the final two; fully residential.

IPMAT Indore RouteMaths MandatoryResidential Program
Prepare for IIFT IPM

Intake

Limited seats each cycle. Confirm the current intake during counselling.

Eligibility Criteria

  • 10+2 in arts, commerce or science with 60% (55% for SC / ST / PwD / Transgender) in the notified years
  • Class 10 with 60% (55% for reserved categories) from the notified year onwards
  • Mathematics / Business Mathematics at +2 level is mandatory
  • Age bar as notified each cycle, with relaxation for reserved categories

Admission Process

  • Shortlisting on IPMAT (IIM Indore) score — apply for IPMAT first and note its registration number
  • Then submit IIFT’s online application quoting the IPMAT registration number

Gender Diversity

Female candidates receive 2 marks in the selection merit (male candidates 0).

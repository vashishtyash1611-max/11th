Program Overview

One Curriculum. Many Possibilities.

At School of Commerce, the Class 11th & 12th curriculum is planned together with competitive exam preparation — so school exams, entrance tests and foundation building reinforce each other instead of competing for time.

  • Class 11th & 12th curriculum coverage
  • Integrated preparation for CLAT, IPM, CUET
  • UPSC Foundation building from school years
  • Mentorship, mock tests and a structured academic roadmap

Academic Curriculum & Roadmap

The Two-Year Roadmap

From the first day of Class 11th to college admission — every stage is planned.

Stage 01 · Class 11 Begins

Class 11

Orientation to the commerce stream, subject selection guidance and setting the two-year goal map with mentors.

Stage 02

Foundation

Core concepts of accountancy, business studies, economics and mathematics — the academic base for both boards and UPSC Foundation.

Stage 03

Skill Building

Reading habits, comprehension, writing expression, aptitude basics and current affairs awareness — trained weekly.

Stage 04

Entrance Preparation

Exam-focused tracks begin for CLAT, IPM, CUET, SAT and SET based on each student’s target list.

Stage 05 · Class 12

Class 12

Board curriculum accelerates in sync with entrance revision — one integrated study plan, mentor-reviewed monthly.

Stage 06

Mock Tests

Full-length board-pattern and entrance mocks with analytics, followed by targeted improvement cycles.

Stage 07 · Outcome

College Admission

Application and form-filling guidance, cutoff awareness and counselling support through admission season.

Overview of CUET UG Exam Pattern

The Common University Entrance Test (CUET) UG, conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA), is a national-level entrance exam for admission to undergraduate programs across 200+ participating universities in India — including central, state, private and deemed universities. The exam provides a standardized and fair evaluation for students from diverse academic backgrounds. CUET UG is conducted in Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode, featuring multiple-choice questions (MCQs) across all sections.

Exam Structure

The CUET UG exam is divided into three main sections — Language Test, Domain-Specific Subjects and General Aptitude Test (GAT). Candidates can select a maximum of five subjects across these sections, which must include at least one language paper. The exam runs in multiple shifts over several days, depending on candidate count and subject combinations. Each subject paper carries 50 MCQs with a uniform duration of 60 minutes.

Section-Wise Details

Section I — Language Test

Assesses proficiency in the chosen language through reading comprehension, vocabulary and grammar. 13 languages offered — Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Odia, Bengali, Assamese, Punjabi, English, Hindi and Urdu. 50 MCQs per language paper · 60 minutes per paper. Candidates can choose up to two languages; the paper is available in the chosen language (and English / Hindi as selected during registration).

Section II — Domain-Specific Subjects

Tests knowledge based on the NCERT Class 12 syllabus for the chosen subject. 23 domain subjects offered — including Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, Accountancy, Economics, History, Political Science and Business Studies. 50 MCQs per subject · 60 minutes per subject. Candidates can choose up to four domain subjects (aligned with university eligibility). Admissions for discontinued subjects — Entrepreneurship, Teaching Aptitude, Fashion Studies, Tourism, Legal Studies, Engineering Graphics — are based on the General Aptitude Test score.

Section III — General Aptitude Test (GAT)

Evaluates general knowledge, current affairs, mental ability, numerical ability and logical reasoning — covering general knowledge including current affairs, general mental ability, numerical ability (basic mathematics up to Class 8), quantitative reasoning (arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, statistics) and logical & analytical reasoning. 50 MCQs · 60 minutes. Optional, depending on university / program requirements.

Subject Selection and University Requirements

  • Select subjects against the eligibility criteria of your target university and program — each university may require specific language, domain and GAT combinations
  • Maximum five subjects — typically one or two languages (Section I), up to four domain subjects (Section II), plus GAT (Section III) where required
  • Refer to official university brochures to confirm subject choices meet admission criteria

Key Changes in CUET

  • Reduced subject options — 37 total (13 languages, 23 domain subjects, 1 General Test), down from 63
  • Discontinued subjects (Entrepreneurship, Teaching Aptitude, Fashion Studies, Tourism, Legal Studies, Engineering Graphics) now admit via the General Aptitude Test score
  • Uniform 60-minute duration for every subject paper
  • Exclusively Computer-Based (CBT) mode — hybrid format discontinued
